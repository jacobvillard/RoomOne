## 🙌 Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and leaders, pledge to make participation in our community a **harassment-free experience** for everyone, regardless of:

- Age  
- Body size  
- Visible or invisible disability  
- Ethnicity  
- Sex characteristics  
- Gender identity and expression  
- Level of experience  
- Education  
- Socio-economic status  
- Nationality  
- Personal appearance  
- Race  
- Religion  
- Sexual identity and orientation

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an **open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy** community.

---

## 📏 Our Standards

### ✅ Positive behavior includes:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward others  
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences  
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback  
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing for mistakes, and learning from them  
- Prioritizing the well-being of the community over individual interests  

### 🚫 Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language, imagery, or advances of any kind  
- Trolling, insults, or derogatory comments  
- Personal or political attacks  
- Public or private harassment  
- Publishing others' private information without permission  
- Any behavior deemed inappropriate in a professional setting  

---

## 🛠️ Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. They may take appropriate and fair action in response to:

- Inappropriate behavior  
- Threatening or offensive content  
- Harmful contributions

Leaders may **remove or reject** comments, commits, code, edits, issues, or other contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## 🌍 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to **all community spaces**—online and offline—and also when an individual is officially representing the community, such as:

- Using an official email address  
- Posting from an official social media account  
- Participating as a designated representative

## 📚 Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders follow these guidelines when determining consequences for violations:

### ⚠️ 1. Correction

**Impact**: Unprofessional or inappropriate language/behavior.

**Consequence**: Private warning with clarity on the violation. A public apology may be requested.

---

### 🟡 2. Warning

**Impact**: Single or repeated minor violations.

**Consequence**: Warning with conditions. Limited interaction with affected individuals or community for a set time.

---

### 🔒 3. Temporary Ban

**Impact**: Serious or sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: Temporary ban from all community interaction. Violation of terms may lead to permanent removal.

---

### 🚫 4. Permanent Ban

**Impact**: Repeated violations, harassment, or aggression toward individuals/groups.

**Consequence**: Permanent removal from the community.
